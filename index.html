Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

woodniu is a Bluetooth tool application for connecting to woodniu motor controllers, reading and adjusting controller parameters.

Data Collection Statement

This app does NOT collect, store, upload or share any personal information.

  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location, device ID or any other personal sensitive data.
  • All Bluetooth communication is only between your mobile phone and the local VESC hardware. Parameter data will never be sent to any remote server.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no account login required in this application.

Permission Explanation

The Bluetooth permission requested by the application is only used to scan and connect to the VESC controller, and will not be used for any other purpose.
